## MAJOR

### M1. Rule C is said to remove the setpoint difference, but the paper's own comfort slope and admission sensitivity exceed the 3-point resolution threshold
- Location: Sec. 8.2 (Cost, Energy and On-Commands), main.tex l.1822–1826; Sec. 7 Statistics, l.1666–1669; C3, l.186–190.
- Quoted: "Each saving is the pooled equalised saving against THERM shifted to the arm's own clipped mean, so no part of it is a setpoint difference" (l.1823–1825); "Pooled differences of less than 3 points between arms are treated as not resolved ... because the occupied-mean staircase and the tuning noise are of that order" (l.1666–1669).
- What is wrong: (a) The Limitations section (l.2501–2504) and the tab:main caption state that "Rule C does not cancel a difference in comfort between arms: along the guard family a 0.01 K warmer mean is worth about 1.3 points". The sentence at l.1824 says the opposite. (b) At 1.3 points per 0.01 K, the 3-point threshold corresponds to about 0.023 K. Rule A, however, admits any candidate within ±0.03 K pooled on the tuning windows (±0.10 K under the fallback), and held-out ΔT̃ ranges from −0.099 to +0.121 K across the tabulated arms. The comfort artefact that the protocol allows is therefore larger than the threshold that is supposed to absorb it. (c) The supplement measures the "tuning noise" directly, and it is larger than 3 points. Refitting under a per-window 0.03 K criterion (Table S2; T1v3_sens.csv) moves TUNED from 12.5/13.6/12.4 to 9.7/12.2/4.0, which is 8.4 points in Taif. It also reverses the sign of F4 in Taif: the guard saves 7.8 (fallback 0.10 K) against TUNED's 4.0. The MPC arms are not refitted under that criterion, so the robustness of F2 and F3 to the admission rule is unknown.
- Evidence: T1v3_sens.csv rows per_window_0.03 (TUNED taif s=3.969, guard taif s=7.763, fit_status pooled_0.10); T1v3_heldout_cth7200.csv (Taif TUNED dT_pooled=+0.121 at fit_status pooled_0.03); E2 (rbrl_e2_v3_riyadh.csv) is essentially the hold policy at 23.80 °C and scores 12.2–14.0% at ΔT̃ +0.08 to +0.13 K, against the guard at 23.60 °C scoring 5.7% at +0.036 K. That is one policy family moving 7 points with mean temperature after equalisation.
- Fix: (1) Delete "so no part of it is a setpoint difference". Replace it with "Rule C removes the first-order energy effect of the mean temperature; a residual dependence remains (Section 10.4)". (2) Justify the 3-point threshold from the measured admission sensitivity (Table S2), or raise it. Otherwise, state plainly that the per-window refit moves TUNED by up to 8.4 points and reverses F4 in Taif. (3) Refit MPC-24 (perfect and persistence) under the per-window criterion and add F2–F4 under that criterion to Table S2, or state that the F2 resolution was not tested for robustness to the admission rule. (4) Add the held-out ΔT̃ difference between arms as a column in tab:stats.

### M2. "Not resolved" (F3) is turned into "requires a perfect forecast", and a descriptive capacity sweep is turned into "requires a light plant"
- Location: C4, l.194–196 ("the receding-horizon controller's margin over the tuned thermostat requires a perfect in-horizon forecast and a light plant"); Sec. 8.3 l.2005–2006 ("The margin over the tuned thermostat therefore requires a perfect in-horizon forecast"); Response letter, Comment 4 and "Overstated conclusions" bullet 7.
- What is wrong: F3 is a null result: absence of a resolved margin is not evidence that the margin is absent. Under the paper's own comfort caveat, the null is also biased against the persistence controller. MPC-24(s) is admitted only at the 0.10 K fallback in Riyadh and Taif, with λ/λ* = 13.09 and 6.22, and its held-out means are 0.087, 0.066 and 0.091 K cooler than TUNED's (−0.076/−0.037/−0.086 against +0.011/+0.029/+0.005). At 1.3 points per 0.01 K that offset exceeds the observed gap, in the direction that penalises MPC. On capacity, the sweep is declared descriptive (l.1666). The margin is still 3.8 points at 7,200 kJ/K, at or above the paper's own 3-point threshold. It vanishes only at 9,600 kJ/K, where the planner's drop coincides with a 0.044 K cooler matched mean and +2.5% electricity, which the text itself flags as a comfort-device effect (l.2043–2046).
- Evidence: T20v3_paired_stats.csv F3 (fit_status_a pooled_0.10 for Riyadh/Taif); T21v3_pers_riyadh.csv / _taif.csv (lam_over_lam_star 13.094 / 6.2176, dT_pooled −0.076 / −0.086); T21v3_riyadh_cth7200.csv (19.80) vs T1v3_heldout_cth7200.csv (16.02); T21v3_riyadh_cth9600.csv (dT −0.044, energy −2.51%).
- Fix: C4 should read: "... whose margin over the tuned thermostat is resolved only with a perfect in-horizon forecast (with a persistence forecast it is not resolved, at a matched mean 0.07–0.09 K cooler than TUNED's), and which shrinks with thermal capacity, from 15.0 points at 2,400 to 3.8 at 7,200 and 0.4 at 9,600 kJ/K". Apply the same change at l.2006, in the letter (Comment 4, Comment 7 and the overstated-conclusions list) and in Conclusions l.2544–2546.

### M3. Sign error in the comfort-offset argument that dismisses the in-rules planner's advantage
- Location: Sec. 8.3 "Inside the rules", l.2073–2076; this argument underpins Remark 5 l.1103–1105, l.2080–2082 and Conclusions l.2559–2563.
- Quoted: "the training-rule runs are 0.04–0.12 K cooler than the guard, which at about 1.3 points per 0.01 K is enough to account for their apparent advantage."
- What is wrong: the paper's slope is "a 0.01 K **warmer** mean is worth about 1.3 points" (l.2502). The training-rule MPC-24 runs are cooler than the guard (ΔT̃ −0.061/−0.003/−0.064 against +0.036/+0.035/+0.056) and yet save more (11.6/14.1/10.0 against 5.7/7.6/11.0). A cooler mean penalises an arm, so the offset makes their advantage larger at matched comfort, not explainable. The sentence is used to support "The room inside the rules is the guard's fixed margins ... not lookahead and not learning". That conclusion then rests only on the guard-rules runs, and those include a Jeddah fit with λ/λ* = 0.60 at the 0.10 K fallback.
- Evidence: T16v3_r23.8-22.4_{riyadh,jeddah,taif}.csv (24 h rows: 11.63/−0.061, 14.06/−0.003, 10.04/−0.064); T1v3_heldout.csv guard rows; T16v3_r23.62-22.4_jeddah.csv (24 h: 4.36, pooled_0.10, λ/λ* 0.6009).
- Fix: delete the "enough to account for" clause. State that, inside the wider training rule set, a 24 h planner exceeds the fitted guard by 5.9 and 6.5 points in Riyadh and Jeddah at a cooler mean, and that this is a comparison across different rule sets. Restrict "neither lookahead nor learning adds" (l.1104–1105, 2081–2082, 2559–2563) to "inside the guard's own fitted thresholds", and note the Jeddah fallback/λ caveat there.

### M4. "PPO converges to the rules' hold policy" is generalised beyond the evidence (Taif seeds; E2)
- Location: Abstract l.137–138; C4 l.197–199; Sec. 5.4 l.1346–1353; Sec. 9.2 l.2183–2192; Conclusions l.2558–2559; Response letter summary and "Overstated conclusions" bullet 5.
- What is wrong: (a) Taif: the guard's fitted ceiling is 23.86. Seeds 43, 44 and 46 all select 23.86 yet save 11.00, 9.62 and 10.05%, and seeds 42 and 45 (23.84) save 8.61 and 9.00%, with energy differences from −0.79% to +0.98%. Only seed 43 is the hold policy. Four of five Taif seeds are not, and all four do worse. The text calls this a spread that "carries no information about learning", but it is direct evidence of non-convergence in one of three cities. (b) E2 (Riyadh only, 3 seeds): the three seeds give three different held-out results (s = 12.24/14.02/12.47; ΔT̃ = +0.078/+0.097/+0.126 K). The run file records their deviation from hold as +0.163, −0.020 and +0.102 on-commands per day and J within −0.3% to +2.6% of hold. They are near hold, not at it. E2 is evaluated at fit_status "fixed", outside Rule A, which contradicts C3 ("applied identically to every arm"). Its λ is set where, by the text's own account, "the λ term is flat in comfort" (l.2188), so E2 is a weak test of a comfort-aware reward.
- Evidence: rbrl_ppo5_v3_taif.csv; T1v3_heldout.csv (Taif guard cap 23.86, s 11.002); rbrl_e2_v3_riyadh.csv (fs_minus_hold, J_hold_mean, J_arm_mean, fit_status=fixed).
- Fix: "In Riyadh and Jeddah the five cost-only seeds reproduce the hold policy (eight of ten to 1e-5); in Taif four of five deviate from it and save 1.0–2.4 points less. E2 (Riyadh, three seeds, not selected by Rule A and not comfort-matched) stays within 0.16 on-commands per day and 2.6% of cost of the hold policy at the training rules." Revise the Abstract, C4, the Conclusions and the letter to match. In C3, name E2 (and MANUAL) as exceptions to "applied identically to every arm".

## MINOR

m1. Holm statement is false. Sec. 7, l.1663–1665: "0.047 is the smallest Holm-adjusted p attainable, reached only when every window in every city agrees in sign". Holm's first step is 3 × p_min, so one city reaches 0.047 regardless of the others. tab:stats F4 shows it: Riyadh and Jeddah reach 0.047 while Taif is 5/2 (T20v3_paired_stats.csv). Fix: "reached by a city only when all seven of its windows agree in sign".

m2. Differences computed from rounded values. l.2035: "margin over TUNED is 15.0, 5.8, 3.8 and 0.4", but tab:stats and the CSV give 5.7 (d_pooled 5.738), and the letter (Comment 2) says 5.7. l.2026: persistence "costs 5.6, 3.5 and 6.1 points" at 24 h, but unrounded values give 5.52, 3.42 and 6.18 (T21v3_*.csv). Fix: compute every quoted difference from unrounded values and use 5.7 throughout.

m3. The bracket ratio mixes scoring rules. Conclusions l.2553–2556 ("69–74% of the best attained schedule ... 59–68% of the provable bound") divide TUNED's Rule-C, simulator-terminal saving by CLV's raw, V_N-terminal saving and by B*. That contradicts the paper's own rule at l.1798–1800 (Rule-C savings are paired only with the arm's own bound). Fix: quote s_TUNED / B_TUNED, and TUNED's raw V_N saving against CLV raw, or drop the ratios.

m4. City-specific nulls are generalised. Remark 5 (l.1094–1105) and Conclusions (l.2559–2563) say the one-step shield "removes" the room the guard leaves. In Taif, D1 vs guard is 12.4 vs 11.0 (1.4 points, not resolved) and F4 Taif is not resolved, so there is no resolved room to remove there. The Remark also omits that the guard-rules MPC-24 falls 3.2 points short in Jeddah (stated only at l.2072). Fix: restrict the claim to Riyadh and Jeddah and mention the Jeddah shortfall in the Remark.

m5. Discussion l.2354–2357: "the switch points that win it move from week to week, which a fixed pair cannot follow and a planner can (TUNED 12.4%, the 24 h controller 16.9%)". This holds only with a perfect forecast. The persistence planner saves 10.8% in Taif, below TUNED. Fix: add "with a perfect forecast".

m6. Band sensitivity is claimed but not run. l.1556 says the band width "is treated as a sensitivity, not assumed (Section 10.3)", while l.2409–2410 says "the band was not swept under the protocol, so no figure is given". Fix: delete the clause at l.1556.

m7. Formulation and step size disagree. Definition 4 (l.930): "H=720 hours for a 30-day month". The study steps at 15 min (N = 672 steps per 168 h window, Algorithm 1). Fix: "H = 2,880 steps of 15 min".

m8. tab:stats hides fit status and comfort offset. F3 compares an arm admitted at the 0.10 K fallback (Riyadh, Taif) with one at 0.03 K, and this is not marked in the table. Fix: add the a/b fit-status superscripts used in tab:main and a ΔT̃_a − ΔT̃_b column.

m9. The setback comparator is not stated (Sec. 8.2 "Occupancy setback", l.1963–1983; tab:layers caption). It is unclear whether TUNED-SB and MPC-SB are equalised against non-setback THERM, in which case the saving includes the policy change of letting the empty house float, or against a setback baseline. Fix: state the comparator in one sentence.

m10. The setpoint result comes from the superseded protocol, yet it appears in the Abstract (l.142–143, qualitatively) and the Conclusions (l.2569–2570, "8–16%"). Sec. 10.3 l.2414–2420 discloses that it is "under the round-3 protocol". This conflicts with "One protocol ... applies to every arm" (l.1599) and with the letter's "every figure was regenerated from it" (letter l.17). Fix: rerun under the v3 protocol, or remove the numbers from the Conclusions and qualify the letter's opening statement.

m11. The no-rules ablation result is at the edge of the search grid. rbrl_norules5_v3.csv: the only admissible no-rules ceiling (Riyadh seed 42) is 23.50, the lower edge of the grid {23.50, ..., 24.00} in Algorithm 1. "No admissible ceiling" for the other nine runs may be a grid-range artefact (l.2174–2178). Fix: extend the grid below 23.50 or state the edge effect.

m12. Provenance of the time-step comfort record. The THERM figures "4.47 occupied degree-hours" at 1 h and "0.025" at 30 min (l.529; letter Comment 8; Table S1(a)) come from T2_timestep.csv, a pre-v3 file whose 15-min rule saving (13.96) is not the paper's. gate_p12.log class H reports "baseline deg-h 10.949" at 60 min for the same condition. Class G also tests feasibility "against a 4.0 K band", which is the superseded [22,26] band. Fix: regenerate THERM's degree-hours per step from v3 code on the held-out windows, state which windows the gate uses, and retire the 4.0 K-band checks from the 188-invariant count (or say they are legacy).

m13. Signed "negative savings" fall inside the not-resolved zone. Abstract l.133–136, l.2386 ("TUNED itself turns negative at half the reference capacity") and Conclusions l.2546–2552. At 2,400 kJ/K and at 30 min, TUNED's fitted points (23.51/22.55 and 23.53/22.55; T1v3_heldout_cth2400.csv, _dt30.csv) are essentially THERM. Their −2.8% and −1.2 to −1.5% are below the 3-point rule and of the order of Rule C's own conservatism (THERM self-scores −0.8% at the reference; not reported at these settings). The 15.0-point margin at 2,400 kJ/K inherits this, and its MPC uses λ/λ* = 7.0. Fix: report THERM's self-score at each sensitivity setting, and say "no resolved saving" rather than "turns negative".

m14. Algorithm 1 vs Sec. 5.4: the terminal pricing of the thermal state at the end of the episode (l.1322–1324) is missing from Phase 1. Fix: add one line to Algorithm 1.

m15. "Compressor on-commands" (Abstract l.125, l.1917, Conclusions l.2537) contradicts Sec. 3.3 (l.645–648), which says on-commands are not established as compressor starts. Fix: use "on-commands" throughout.

## Response letter: truthfulness and completeness
- Comment 2 (capacity): answered with a justified refusal of the reviewer's replacement sentence; the "lower bound" claim is gone from main.tex (grep confirms). The quoted margin "5.7" disagrees with the manuscript's "5.8" (m2).
- Comment 3 (split): fully answered. Dates, no overlap, no validation set and the seasonal treatment all match l.1584–1598 and Algorithm 1.
- Comment 6 (tariff): fully answered. Headline at the tariff in force, the 2027 rate repeated, the four-tier schedule labelled stress-test.
- Comment 8 (time step): answered. The exact update and the 5/15/30-min sweep under refit, with comfort reported. The comfort figures' provenance needs fixing (m12).
- Comment A (language) and the editor's instruction: largely met. Three statements in the letter are not accurate as worded: (i) "E2 ... converges to the rule layer's hold policy, as the cost-only agent does" (M4); (ii) "requires a perfect in-horizon forecast and a light plant" (M2); (iii) "every figure was regenerated from it" (m10). Its claim that the room inside the rules is not lookahead depends on the sign error in M3.

## Statistical-reporting completeness
Adequate. Exact tests, HL estimates with exact 95.3% CIs, Holm within pre-declared families, the n = 7 floor disclosed, and effect sizes lead the p-values. Two gaps: the resolution threshold is not calibrated to the measured admission sensitivity (M1), and comfort offsets are not shown beside the paired contrasts (m8). No sign of selective reporting; nulls (F3, F4 Taif, multi-zone) are reported.

## Verdict (methodology dimension): NOT READY
All four MAJORs are corrections to wording or inference that the existing CSVs support. One optional robustness rerun (MPC-24 under the per-window admission criterion) would settle M1.
